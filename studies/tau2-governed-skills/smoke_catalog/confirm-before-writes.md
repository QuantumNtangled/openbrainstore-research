---
kind: checklist
title: Confirm before any write
---

1. Authenticate the customer the way the policy requires before looking anything up.
2. Gather every detail the change needs from the tools, not from memory.
3. Read the exact change back to the customer (what changes, any amount, which payment
   method) and wait for an explicit "yes".
4. Make one write call per confirmed change. Never act on a detail the customer did not confirm.
5. After the write succeeds, tell the customer what changed.
